ROAD GUNNER — VEHICLE SCENERY CRUSH, 3 NOVEMBER 2026

Baseline source: ece4240. Unity 6000.6.4f1 / URP 17.6.0.
Request: small cacti, crates and similar props break when a car drives into them;
large rocks, cliffs and buildings remain solid.

The existing visuals were retained. Original Arcade chunk geometry was replayed
without mutation and compared with every saved position, normal, UV and index:
1067 primary items, 29 chunks, 207432 vertices. Eight mixed Concept shoulder
recipes were matched triangle by triangle against their original member meshes;
79 placed compositions yielded 354 independently classified members. Standalone
legacy and Concept roadside props were included. Gameplay targets, actors,
reward caches, upgrades, workshop equipment and loot were excluded.

The saved scene has 208 mutable source MeshFilters and 688 soft item probes.
Supported soft families include 168 cacti, 24 wooden supply crates, 4 metal crates,
76 individual Arcade barrels, 24 fuel-drum groups, dead branches, tyre/scrap props,
loose pipes, plates and bones. Grass tufts, dry scrub, flat pebbles, dunes and
ash drifts remain surface detail. Authoring.json gives the complete classification.

Large scenery has 209 new compact, exact static MeshColliders, plus 3 reused hard
colliders: 125 Boulder entries, 46 rock-spire groups, 20 hulls and 21 girders.
Boulders are hard at world height>=0.32m or horizontal size>=1m; smaller pebbles
remain ground detail. Existing 136 protected solid colliders were preserved.
Collider-based navigation baking used the existing configuration. Three formerly
nonblocking Boulder compositions entered the car clearance envelope after adding
solid collision. Their complete shoulder instances were moved about 3 m away
from the workshop and upper Warlord lane; their original local mesh geometry,
materials and hard collision were retained. Authoring.json records all old/new
poses and exact offsets. The roads, route markers and arena geography did not move.
All 14 complete navigation routes, their 736 full-car centreline samples and the
actual workshop driving opening passed before saving the clearance migration.
A separate editor reload verified saved metadata, original visuals and static flags.

Runtime: ArenaDecorationCrush caches the player and living Buggies, performs
non-allocating overlap/swept-box queries with rotation substeps and checks actual
point velocity>=0.25m/s and ground support. Layer 12 RG_SceneryCrush is excluded
from the saved player/Buggy weapon masks. Props are query shapes; the vehicle
moves through them as their visible geometry breaks. Large solids are Default
layer geometry, so they stop both chassis and shots.

DecorationCrushChunk lazily clones the affected source instance and degenerates
only the selected index ranges, retaining all neighbouring and hard geometry.
Original shared mesh assets stay unchanged. Mutable renderers are excluded from
static batching so the same operation works in a packaged player. A prewarmed
pool of 96 cosmetic pieces emits 6-8 coloured fragments, dust and a quiet existing
impact cue. Pieces have no colliders/masses, last roughly 1.1-1.4 s and shrink away.
No HP, damage, kills, upgrades, Scrap or Metal Gear are created by decorative props.

RunSerial restores indices/probes and clears debris on Restart. Pause/final
states stop processing. VehicleController adds only a pose-reset serial; Recover
and Restart cannot sweep a false destruction bridge across a teleport. Existing
tracked speed/torque/combat settings were retained.

Tools / Road Gunner / Scenery / Install Vehicle Crush Collisions Only

Read-only: RoadGunner.Editor.DecorationCollisionInstaller.DumpManifestBatch
One-time narrow clearance repair: DecorationCollisionInstaller.RepairRouteClearanceBatch
The narrow installer verifies the saved source catalog before authoring and uses
it for repeat installation. It does not reapply the full art/world/minimap passes.
Intentional changes to source meshes or poses must be reconciled with that catalog.

Props.txt records actual input-driven physical drives using copies of production
chunks with one selected item enabled on a disclosed temporary course. Its 26
checks cover original layers, selected-range preservation, repeated contact,
HP/rewards, near miss, Recover, states, Restart, a real authored MeshCollider and
Boulder/cliff/bunker physical impacts. Screenshots are actual native camera/HUD
captures of that fixture, not a natural arena playthrough. Other native suites,
WebGL artifact identity and public browser evidence are recorded separately.
