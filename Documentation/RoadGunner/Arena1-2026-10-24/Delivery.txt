ARENA 1

2 vehicle/elite Buggies; 1 Monster mini-boss; 1 final Boss Raider. Total 16 enemies.
Finite authored encounters; random encounter groups and automatic replenishment
are disabled in this Arena. Unused reserve pools remain available to the tools.
Progression: southern start, early Raider pockets, central encounters, east road
Buggy, mixed east camp encounters, Monster in northeast open yard, west boss arena.
Monster world XYZ: (175.01, 0.06, 30.05), existing Ashmaw prefab,330 HP.
Boss Raider world XYZ: (3.54, 0.08, 120.21),520 HP, existing Raider combat class and
Veteran art, scaled 1.6. Committed .85s warned strike,16 damage,10 Metal Gear reward.
Existing boss health panel shows the nearest live boss and attack state.
Only Boss Raider death invokes completion; accumulated and pending earned gear
is settled into the result before the existing overlay freezes the run.
Installation.txt records every final nav-sampled enemy/station position.

BALANCE
Player max forward speed: 18 -> 6 m/s; exact multiplier 1/3.
Reverse: 7 -> 2.333333 m/s; exact multiplier 1/3.
Drive torque 3100 -> 2400; steering rate 125, steering curves, brakes, suspension
and wheel friction retained. Ram activation threshold 6 -> 2 m/s.
Base bullet damage remains 15 in tuning; central outgoing multiplier .35 produces
5.25 damage at ATK 1. Actual gun checks:9 shots for45 HP Raider;20 shots for105 HP Buggy.
The same central multiplier applies to player ram damage; enemy gun damage is
independent. Configuration lives in Levels/Greenwood/Arena1 assets and Inspector.

UPGRADES
One-use proximity stations, radius 3.5 m, no extra interaction button.
ATTACK +1: attack level 1 -> 2; +20% of reduced base damage,5.25 ->6.3 per bullet.
HEALTH +1: health level 1 -> 2; existing VehicleHealth maximum 100 ->120 HP, granting
the additional capacity while retaining existing damage. Existing workshop heals
against the upgraded maximum. No parallel health or permanent progression system.
Attack station XYZ: (-37.12, 0.06, 8.84).
Health station XYZ: (86.62, 0.06, 19.45).
Stations have readable labels, consumed state, pickup audio/VFX hook and HUD toast.
Levels and stations reset on restart; ATK/HP stay visible in a compact HUD pill.

MINIMAP
Top-right 338x338 logical map, within the existing portrait/safe-area Canvas.
Actual LevelLayout gameplayBounds normalized into UI coordinates; no extra camera
or RenderTexture. Cached references rebuild at run reset, refresh at .08s; no
per-frame scene searches or managed allocations in marker movement.
Player direction, Raiders, moving Buggies, Monster, Boss Raider, Attack/Health
stations and existing workshop have distinct procedural icons. Dead enemies and
consumed upgrades disappear.20 live markers at the start of the saved Arena.
Optional MAP KEY opens a readable legend; it is hidden by default and closes
after 8 seconds, on pause/completion, and on restart, preserving the combat view.

WEB / PUBLICATION
Unity 6000.6.4f1 / URP 17.6.0; output WebBuild/.
Gzip with JavaScript Decompression Fallback (.unityweb), single-threaded WebGL,
128 MB initial/512 MB maximum WASM memory, existing Mobile quality default.
Responsive portrait canvas, dynamic viewport height, disabled page scroll/gesture
handling on the canvas, device pixel ratio capped at 2, keyboard and touch input.
Manual canvas resizing retains the last positive size during viewport transitions.
APP_UI_EDITOR_ONLY is enabled for WebGL, matching the existing Android/Standalone
configuration, so unused App UI editor tooling cannot initialize mobile touch state.
Build entry: RoadGunner.EditorTools.WebBuild.Build with -buildTarget WebGL.
Acceptance entry: RoadGunner.Editor.Arena1Verification.Run with -buildTarget StandaloneOSX on this Mac, without -quit/-nographics.
Final build/push/deployment/browser verification details are in Publication.txt.
