Before and after
- The pre-change saved walker already passed 33 instantaneous-movement checks.
  Full input, release, reversal and Space therefore retain that control contract;
  this pass improves support validation and presentation rather than adding an
  acceleration ramp. Wheel contacts now require a sufficiently upward normal
  and matching ground below the wheel. A falling brush against a wall cannot
  become ground support. Compound sweeps retain wall stopping/sliding and
  movement away from contact; vertical fall and suspension remain physical.
- Previously feet advanced from local stride without a persistent world stance
  anchor. Supported stance now holds a measured world foot position, replans
  on stops/reversals and drives bounded, damped pelvis/torso reactions. The same
  accepted foot plant produces one sole impression, dust burst and mechanical
  footfall. The player tyre-roll/skid layers are suppressed. Landing feedback
  follows a real fall followed by stable support; reset and idle motion do not
  invent locomotion plants.
- Cannon aim/fire previously ran before the late gait had moved its mount.
  Gait now resolves at 12000, cannon at 12100. One immutable actual shot snapshot
  supplies muzzle pose, forward hitscan, tracer, flash and hit result. World aim
  persists across chassis reversals. A private runtime barrel mesh recoils and
  returns without changing shared source geometry or the collision body.
- Harpoon resolves at 12200, after the actual moving mount. Its eight-point
  world-space cable runs from the current winch to the actual hook/reserved Gear;
  launch slack tightens during powered pull. Launch/dock reactions and distinct
  launch/latch/dock cues accompany a retrieval-only winch loop. Disabling an
  already reserved Gear visual now releases ownership by owner/slot/generation,
  fixing the former stuck-reservation case. Pause retains a valid frozen pull;
  invalid targets, Recover and terminal/reset transitions clean up the hook and
  audio. A Restart inside the real award callback cannot leave a stale dock.
