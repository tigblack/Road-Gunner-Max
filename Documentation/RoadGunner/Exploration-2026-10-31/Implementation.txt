ROAD GUNNER — EXPLORATION REVISION
31 October 2026, Asia/Yerevan
Unity 6000.6.4f1 / URP 17.6.0. This revision extends the current Unity/WebGL game.

DESIGN
Four wasteland regions: Dust Flats, Salvage Yard, Rustworks, Ash Ridge.
Connected roads, bypasses, combat clearings and optional secret branches extend
the existing open Arena 1. Locations of the 16 enemies and the original workshop
remain authored references. Warlord still ends the run; Monster is optional.
The player's wheel-driven controller and vehicle tuning are preserved.

Five destructible supply caches have 10.5 durability (two baseline 5.25 bullets).
Rewards are 2 x +5% outgoing damage, 1 x +10% additional ram, 2 x +5 flat maximum HP.
Percent damage cache bonuses add together then multiply level-scaled damage;
the ram bonus multiplies that result only for ram. Flat HP preserves damage:
70/100 becomes75/105. Both original free level terminals remain.
Three nearby secret chests grant 3 Scrap each and up to 15 / 20 / 15 HP repair.
They appear on the map within 20 m and remain revealed until Restart.
Secrets use actual distance and line of sight; they do not block bullets.
Caches do not grant enemy kills, spawn another pickup, or pay twice.
Scrap from secrets participates in the same earned/collected accounting.
Unclaimed caches/secrets are not auto-awarded when Warlord dies.

Optional counterplay is assigned to actual enemy components:
- Rust Vulture Buggy: ram damage multiplier 1.25.
- Warlord: bullet multiplier 1.2 only during recovery after a committed strike.
Other damage, player input and physical contact response remain unchanged.
The boss HUD exposes the actual recovery damage window.
No new monetization, respawn wait or permanent stat save is introduced.

DATA AND EDITING
Assets/RoadGunner/Levels/Greenwood/Exploration/Exploration layout.asset:
Editable routes/widths, pockets, region colors/names/hints, cache definitions,
recovery laybys and baseline transforms/materials for idempotent reauthoring.
Assets/RoadGunner/Levels/Greenwood/Exploration/Exploration tuning.asset:
Reward defaults, reveal/collection distances, LOS mask and counterplay factors.
Cache definitions can override default amounts and durability.
Hint templates read actual tuning values; the HUD does not invent stat numbers.

To apply edited layout outside Play Mode:
Tools / Road Gunner / Arena1 / Install Exploration Arena
Batch: RoadGunner.Editor.ExplorationArenaInstaller.InstallBatch
The installer opens/saves the main scene, rebuilds the connected road mesh and
surface texture, refreshes navigation, regenerates the 512 x 512 minimap and authors
exploration HUD. It never invokes legacy rebalance installers with old speeds.
Protected cliffs/gates/workshop are retained. Blockers may be moved intact to
verified shoulders, with original transforms stored in the layout asset.
Actual driving and clearance evidence belongs in acceptance reports.
