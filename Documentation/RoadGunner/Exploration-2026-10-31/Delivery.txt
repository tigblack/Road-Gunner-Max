ROAD GUNNER — EXPLORATION REVISION, 31 OCTOBER 2026
Unity 6000.6.4f1 / URP 17.6.0

RESULT
The current Unity Arena 1 now has four wasteland regions, 14 connected routes,
12 turning/combat pockets, five breakable supply caches and three secrets.
Alternate routes give room to approach encounters and seek optional rewards.
The workshop remains a physically open drive-through repair hub. Existing
cliffs, gates and workshop are preserved; eight obstructing props moved intact
to checked shoulders. Navigation and the 512 x 512 minimap were rebuilt.

This adapts exploration, visible rewards and enemy counterplay to Road Gunner.
It does not reproduce Butcher Hero's unverified level layouts or stat tables.
The current task extends one finite Arena 1; it does not create a campaign.

Bonuses are for this run: two +5% damage caches, one +10% ram cache and two
+5 maximum/current HP caches. Three secrets each grant 3 Scrap and up to
15 / 20 / 15 HP repair. Duplicate claims, late rewards and stale callbacks after
Restart are rejected. HP gains retain damage already taken. Enemies have gun
priority over supply caches. Distant secrets are hidden on the map until found.
The original free terminals and Metal Gear/harpoon accounting remain.

Rust Vulture takes 25% extra ram damage. Warlord takes 20% extra bullet damage
during recovery after its committed strike. Actual tuning supplies the region
hints and the boss recovery HUD. Warlord still clears the arena immediately;
Monster is optional and the finite roster stays at 16 enemies.

The player controller, vehicle tuning and per-arena handling asset are byte-for-
byte unchanged from the preceding wheel-physics release. Driving remains a
dynamic Rigidbody with four WheelColliders: steering arcs, traction, suspension,
inertial coasting and real impact response. Recover/Restart retain explicit resets.

OPEN / EDIT / BUILD
Open Assets/RoadGunner/Scenes/Level01_Greenwood.unity and press Play.
WASD/arrows or floating joystick request a nose-first travel direction. Leave
room for the physical turning arc. Space brakes; Escape pauses; R recovers.

Edit Assets/RoadGunner/Levels/Greenwood/Exploration/Exploration layout.asset
and Exploration tuning.asset. Then, outside Play Mode:
Tools / Road Gunner / Arena1 / Install Exploration Arena
Batch: RoadGunner.Editor.ExplorationArenaInstaller.InstallBatch
The installer refreshes geometry, navigation, map and the exploration HUD.
For a deliberate legacy HUD rebuild, apply Anton feedback before exploration.

Build: Tools / Road Gunner / Web / Build Mobile Web
Batch: RoadGunner.EditorTools.WebBuild.Build with -buildTarget WebGL.
Output: WebBuild/. The current task publishes this Unity player, not an Unreal
HTML5 build. The Unreal/Godot summary files and local research were not changed.
