MAP MEASUREMENTS
The checkpoint of the current implemented Greenwood level was the only expansion baseline. The 2.5 multiplier was applied once to horizontal coordinates and terrain geometry, not to a shared scene root.

Terrain area increases exactly 6.25 times. The connected walkable envelope is approximately 2.52 × 2.58 times its previous dimensions: ordinary blockers and agent erosion retain their original physical size, so rebaked walkable boundaries do not scale mathematically exactly. These NavMesh measurements describe the connected walkable envelope, not a guarantee that a car can occupy every NavMesh triangle.
The terrain is rotated 45 degrees in the world, which explains the difference between its local dimensions and its world bounding box. The overview is aligned with the main road.

The recognizable main north/south road, west branch and arena, southwest camp, northeast camp, northern salvage area, locked exits, gantry landmark and bunker approach remain in their original relationships. Major paths have widened: nominal main road 18.75 m, southwest spur 12.5 m, northeast approach 11.25 m. Dunes stay shallow (up to approximately 0.24 m); road corridors and the service apron are flattened.
Terrain collision, expanded perimeter collision, navigation, spawn anchors and recovery markers were rebuilt or repositioned. The existing FollowCamera has no separate camera-bound system; its tracking, 16-unit orthographic size and 50/45-degree rotation remain unchanged.

ART AND ENEMIES
Reused the existing wasteland raider and buggy prefabs, original beveled geometry, sand texture and suitable original material sources. New environment assets are in:
Assets/RoadGunner/Levels/WastelandRestored/

Fantasy props were replaced by corrugated fences, rusty containers, lookouts, wrecks, utility poles, metal gates, scrap piles, tire stacks, fuel drums, tool racks and salvaged industrial landmarks. The stone arch becomes a gantry and the sealed entrance becomes an industrial bunker. Warm sand, rust, charcoal steel, faded paint, dirty rubber and concrete form the palette.
Small decorative props do not have wheel-catching solid colliders. Major blockers use simple collision volumes. Extra travel wrecks were moved off the widened junctions after actual driving revealed an obstruction.

The original 13 encounter roles and their existing tuning assets are retained with the original wasteland humanoid prefab. Six transit raiders are added in three deliberately placed pairs. Active enemy cap stays 30. Existing buggy reserve prefabs remain available; this scene did not have authored buggy encounters, so none were invented. Enemy animations, hitboxes, aiming points, damage and death/loot behavior use the existing systems.

VEHICLE AND SYSTEM PRESERVATION
Player size, controller, input, inertia, suspension, combat, health, loot, camera tracking and portrait HUD systems were not replaced. No multiplier was applied to speed, weapon range, aggro, health or healing radius.
Baseline file hashes show 677 existing non-metadata files unchanged, no missing baseline files, and only these baseline assets modified:

- its Terrain mesh;
- its Terrain baseline mesh;

New repair code and environment assets were added. The scene HUD layout is retained, with a repair-status label and updated sector wording.

AUTO REPAIR WORKSHOP
The old Green Pool/fountain component is absent from the finished scene. Its existing landmark location became a drive-through workshop, repositioned with the expanded layout.
World center: (0, 0, -42.43) m.
Find the object in Hierarchy by searching: AUTO REPAIR WORKSHOP
Component: RepairWorkshop
Code: Assets/RoadGunner/Scripts/RepairWorkshop.cs

Inspector settings:
healingRadius = 10 m
maximumHealthFractionPerSecond = 0.1 (10% of the existing maximum HP per scaled second)
At the existing 100 maximum HP, this is 10 HP/second.

The distance check uses the vehicle root in the horizontal plane. It calls the existing VehicleHealth.Heal, respects GameSession.Playing and Time.deltaTime, clamps to max HP, and never revives a destroyed vehicle. It requires no button, currency or menu. Existing incoming damage still applies. A nearest-zone selection plus a per-health/per-frame guard prevents overlapping zones or child colliders from multiplying healing.
A dashed ground ring marks the service radius. A small repair icon and REPAIRING text beside the HP number appear only during actual healing. Pause, leaving the radius, death and reaching full HP stop them.
The side walls define the workshop; enter through either open end. The service apron, approach, turn and departure were driven with the unchanged car. Recovery now places the car on the clear service apron.

VERIFICATION
43 final recorded checks pass. Full evidence is in road-gunner-validation.txt and the project Documentation/RoadGunner/Wasteland-Restore-2026-10-10/ directory.

Actual Play Mode checks include main road, southwest camp, west branch/arena, northeast route, east road, bunker approach, veteran pocket and workshop entry/turn/exit. All passing route samples maintained four grounded wheels and an upright vehicle. Locked north and west gates blocked the actual car.
All 19 authored enemies spawned on navigation. Thirteen key area samples had complete paths from the spawn. A live raider routed around a wreck with a five-corner path and working limb animation. The unchanged turret killed a restored raider in three shots; scrap pickup, harpoon and HUD count worked.
Repair was checked for entry, departure, repeated entry, overlap, multiple child colliders, incoming damage, full HP, pause/resume, death and restart. At measured 30 and 60 FPS, healing matched elapsed scaled time within 0.03 HP. Half-speed game time also gave the expected half-rate per real second.
Final scene audit: zero missing scripts, render meshes, materials or collision meshes; current ground mesh matches its collider; navigation is not stale. Scene is saved and not dirty. Final Console sample has zero current errors, warnings or compilation failures, with no new captured errors after the final validation cursor.
Historical tooling output includes a resolved early compilation error and a Unity Pipeline transport timeout during an overlapping diagnostic request; these are not current gameplay errors.

LIMITATIONS
Verification was performed in the macOS Unity Editor, not on a mobile-device build. Mobile GPU/thermal performance and long-session balancing remain unprofiled. Playability evidence covers the authored routes, landmark approaches and maneuvering areas; it is not an exhaustive test of every square meter against every possible collision angle. The expanded level deliberately keeps open desert travel space and the existing enemy cap.
