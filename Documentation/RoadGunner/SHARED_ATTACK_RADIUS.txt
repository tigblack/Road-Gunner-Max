ROAD GUNNER — SHARED ATTACK RADIUS AND BUGGY GUNS
2026-10-07

REQUEST IMPLEMENTED
Rust Vulture buggies shoot the player. Both vehicle attack radii are 10 Unity
units (metres), measured horizontally between chassis centres. This matches
the player ground circle exactly, independent of muzzle offset or elevation.
The radius comes from one CombatTuning.range value, not two copied settings.
The saved scenes are RoadGunner_Prototype and Level01_Wasteland.

PLAY
Player fire remains automatic. An unobstructed target within the circle is
eligible even when a portrait screen edge crops it. The first raycast hit
still decides impact; scenery blocks either gun. Enemy weapon mask includes
world and player, and excludes the buggy's own body. Player driving, joystick
heading, keyboard reverse, player ramming and pooled rewards are retained.
The later ranged-tactics update removes enemy ram attacks and adds gear loot
and explosion feedback; see RANGED_ENEMIES_AND_GEAR.txt.

BUGGY WEAPON
Real 3D rusty roof mounting, rotating bearing, dark steel receiver/barrel,
ammunition box and cream accent preserve the Scrap Rush wasteland palette.
Yaw turns independently through 360 degrees. Gun pitch follows the target.
Default cadence: 2 rounds/s, 5 damage per actual hit, 0.8 s spawn/resume warmup.
Bullets use the existing bounded tracer/impact pools. Hostile tracers are
orange; the buggy has its own short muzzle flash. Gun death/pause/reset gates
prevent damage after destruction or while the session is paused/defeated.
Enemy gun balance is editable in Settings/Enemy buggy.asset; its range always
reads the player's shared Combat and population.asset setting.

RANGE DISPLAY
An always-visible pale outline and faint transparent interior follow the
player's horizontal position and real drivable terrain. The circle sits just
above the sand, takes no collision/input, and is depth-tested behind world
objects. It uses one dynamic mesh and shared transparent material. The
current close portrait camera is preserved, so lateral circle edges can be
cropped by the screen. The logical radius remains 10 m in every direction.
The circle hides on defeat and returns on restart. Pausing keeps it visible.

Mobile convention reference: Supercell's official Squad Busters battle and
June update descriptions use a squad attack circle and targets near its centre:
https://support.supercell.com/squad-busters/en/articles/battles-in-squad-busters.html
https://squadbusters.supercell.com/en/blog/game-update/june-update-patch-notes/
The ground-circle presentation is an original implementation, without copied
art assets or added on-screen technical labels.
