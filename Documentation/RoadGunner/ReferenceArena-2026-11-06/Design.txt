ROAD GUNNER — REFERENCE-INSPIRED ARENA 1 REDESIGN
6 November 2026 (Asia/Yerevan)

User direction
Rebuild the actual playable first Unity level using the researched Butcher Hero /
likely Devour topology, adapted to Road Gunner's armed vehicle and wasteland.
The user corrected the requested widening from80% to approximately50%.

Scale interpretation
The public footage establishes topology and relative placement, not real metres.
This is an authored adaptation, not a measured 1:1 map or verified current2.1 copy.
Width multiplier1.5 applies to a normalized authoring plan and its road widths.
The authored map bounds W[-70,70] ×[-58,66] are350m east-west by310m north-south;
1W=2.5m. The normalized east-west basis is233.333m, an authoring convention rather
than a claim about the reference world's dimensions. Solid boundary bands and
closed-exit previews occupy part of those bounds. Actors and props keep their
own scale; neither vehicle physics nor combat ranges are multiplied by1.5.

Topology and Road Gunner adaptation
- Southern start and direct approach to a four-arm central junction W(0,8).
- Main north-south road30m; cross road27m; local connections18m or wider.
- Southeast healing pool becomes our visible green free-repair workshop W(22,-14),
  with an actual north-south drive-through,10m radius and10% maxHP/second unchanged.
- The reference's southern stone portal becomes a sealed industrial landmark;
  no teleportation or second playable level is introduced.
- Southwest guard enclosure W(-34,-24) becomes a Raider/Buggy salvage checkpoint.
- West field W(-28,-4) contains a separate accessible Raider group.
- Northeast tent camp W(29,28) becomes a wasteland camp with Raiders and a Buggy;
  the large reference encounter is represented by our optional Ashmaw at W(40,38).
- The northwest armed encounter is adapted as our existing Warlord at W(-40,25).
  Warlord remains inside the boundaries; its death remains the clear condition.
- Three closed exits at W(-62,8), W(62,8), W(0,58), joined to solid boundary flanks.
  They are geographic landmarks and barriers, not a newly invented quest system.
- Short local connections give the car approaches and space to manoeuvre.

Preserved gameplay
Use the existing12 Raiders,2 Buggies,1 Ashmaw and1 Warlord objects and source art.
Do not activate the old runtime wave/pool spawn arrays to make decorative groups.
Retain the current vehicle/tracks, controls, camera, HUD, music, base combat tuning,
upgrades, five supply crates, three secrets and15 eligible death-site respawns.
Historical reference enemy HP and damage are not imported as modern balance.

Implementation and acceptance
New versioned authoring source and explicit environment replacement; old baseline
geometry/collision catalogs describe their historical release and are retained.
Regenerate actual navigation and the baked map from the new saved scene.
Validate full-car route clearance, all encounter/reward approaches, three physical
gates, real input-driven crossing/turning/repair and ordinary run/reset behavior.
Acceptance claims and measurements belong in the final Delivery/PlayMode reports,
not in this pre-implementation design record. No WebGL build/publication requested.
