ROAD GUNNER — MUSCLE PICKUP + COMPANION HARPOON
Implemented 2026-10-08 in the existing project; the game and maps were not rebuilt.

PLAY
Open Assets/RoadGunner/Scenes/RoadGunner_Prototype.unity or Level01_Wasteland.unity.
Press Play. The existing automatic roof gun shoots enemies. Human enemies drop
one Metal Gear, enemy buggies drop one gear stack worth three Scrap. Drive within
8 m of a landed gear and the rear companion automatically retrieves it. Normal
close-range driving pickups still work and award the same existing currency.

DROP / COLLECTION FLOW
Raider.Hit -> GameSession.RaiderDefeated -> EnemyDefeated(gear:true).
EnemyBuggy.Hit -> same EnemyDefeated(gear:true), with existing value 3.
Both call the original PickupPool.DropGear and retain its 0.6 s airborne arc.
Landed items remain in the same pool until collected; overflow still merges value.
Harpoon scans the 60-slot arrays at a throttled interval for the nearest landed,
active unreserved Metal Gear inside its ground-plane radius. It reserves one
slot and generation, turns the pivot, launches the reusable visual tip, connects,
then moves the pickup/tip toward the rear collection point. A two-point LineRenderer
joins the current moving muzzle and the world-space tip. Collection uses the SAME
PickupPool collection method, GameSession.AddScrap and pickup audio as normal
collection. HudView's existing Changed subscription updates immediately.
Claims prevent competing collectors or driving pickups from claiming the same
in-flight item. Restart invalidates all handles and resets visuals. Disable/recover
releases the claim. Pause freezes the sequence and resumes it without extra reward.
There are no pickup-specific Update loops, extra pickup bodies/colliders, scene
searches during collection, projectile spawning/destruction or new enemy damage.

PLACEHOLDERS / REMAINING WORK
Characters are static posed meshes without a skeleton. Their control grip pose
stays fixed while the launcher pivots; final arm/head animation and launch/recoil
animation remain art work. The harpoon uses the existing simple spike mesh as its
flying tip and a basic cable; no final bespoke VFX were required for this prototype.
Transparent glazing and all other materials are simple placeholders without new
textures. Roof gun collision/cover behavior retains the existing hitscan code.
In-editor driving was verified; larger body/wheelbase may require further handling
balancing on authored routes. No Android/iOS device build or device GPU profiling
has been performed. Existing Level01 reference-layout limitations are unchanged.
