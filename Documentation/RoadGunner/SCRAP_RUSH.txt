SCRAP RUSH — implemented art direction
Completed 2026-10-06 (Asia/Yerevan), Unity 6000.6.4f1 / URP.

Open Assets/RoadGunner/Scenes/RoadGunner_Prototype.unity again, then press Play.
The art pass is saved in the actual scene, meshes, materials, textures and prefabs.
Tools > Road Gunner > Build Prototype reproduces the Scrap Rush version.

Visual changes
- Warm golden sand and a subtle procedural wind-brushed texture.
- Red hood, cream side armor, teal windows, paint scuffs and armor bolts.
- Beveled hard surfaces, larger visible tires and chunky tread cleats.
- Cartoon raiders with larger heads, red wraps/scarves, goggles, gloves,
  harnesses, belt pouches and raised crowbars; original movement rig retained.
- Rose sandstone clusters, corrugated metal fencing, dry grass and scrap piles.
- Closer portrait camera, warm directional key, cool fill and URP grading.
- Restyled rounded HUD panels and explicit Pause icon geometry.
- Soft dust at the wheels and a fixed-capacity mesh of fading tire imprints.
- Small environment dressing combined by material; shared mesh/material assets.

Verification
Actual batch Unity Play mode, graphics enabled, in an isolated copy of the project.
Both suites completed with no failing checks: 16 joystick/control checks and
16 physics/combat/session checks, plus each suite's completion marker.
Includes all eight stick directions, moving U-turn, touch cancellation, S reverse,
acceleration, braking, suspension, tire slip, pointer routing, pause, recovery,
blocked shots, damage/rewards, pickup capacity, defeat and three restarts.
The U-turn test ends when the nose reaches its requested heading, rather than
continuing to accelerate straight into later arena obstacles after the turn.
Two art rocks were moved away from the starting maneuver area during validation.
No missing renderer materials, unsupported shaders, C# errors or shader errors.
All four dust emitters played and produced particles while driving.
Unity's cloned SearchDatabase emitted one unrelated startup index exception.
Physical Android/iOS devices and mobile frame rate have not been verified.
