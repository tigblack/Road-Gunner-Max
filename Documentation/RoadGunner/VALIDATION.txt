Test scope and limitations:
The endurance harness steers through the real production input/physics/combat
systems and uses Recover when blocked. Damage is disabled for that stress run
so it can last five minutes; normal health, melee and defeat are tested separately.

phone. Synthetic multi-pointer events cannot certify a physical touchscreen.
No Android or iOS player was built. Only MacStandaloneSupport and WebGLSupport
are installed under this Unity Editor's PlaybackEngines directory. Android and
iOS support modules, device testing and iOS signing remain external setup tasks.
Mobile 30/60 FPS targets and real-device multitouch are explicitly UNVERIFIED.
