JOYSTICK
FloatingJoystick starts at the exact initial screen touch converted to ring-parent
local space, with no border inset clamp. Its origin stays fixed during that drag.
Ring is hidden while inactive. Finger/mouse/pen ownership is retained until release,
cancel, device removal, pause/focus loss or component disable, including off-area
release. Another finger cannot take over the active steering pointer.
The existing lower-left gameplay control area is retained; any point inside it
can start a new gesture. Thumb displacement/radius becomes a desired screen vector.
DriveInput converts it using camera right/forward projected onto the ground plane.
Touch and WASD/arrows use the same desired-direction model, including down180deg.
Dead zone.12 and response exponent1.25 are exposed in Inspector.
Web template prevents page scrolling/selection/context menu/dragging on canvas,
captures active pointers and preserves native Unity input events. Release/cancel
and focus loss continue through the production InputSystem/UGUI path.

WEAPON RANGE
Actual automatic weapon range remains10m. Previous almost-white fill alpha.035,
thin edge alpha.78 and weak brown contrast were hard to see over sand. An additional
material alpha.27 reduced the effective old fill/edge to.00945/.2106; that material
multiplier is now white/alpha1, so the requested colors and opacity reach the shader.
New cyan fill alpha.07, bright cyan-white boundary alpha.98, width.16m and dark
navy contrast stroke width.14m on either side. Existing ground-conforming reusable
mesh, exact vehicle centre and turret.Range source remain; no new camera/collider.
Ground feedback renders before the UGUI HUD/map, so its circle cannot obscure UI.
