ART DIRECTION — SCRAP RUSH (2026-10-06)
Based on the selected cartoon 3D concept: golden sand, red paint, cream armor,
smoky teal glass, rose sandstone and crimson raider scarves.
The scene uses real 3D meshes, lighting and VFX. The concept is not a background.
Beveled vehicle details, chunky tires, bolt heads, hood scuffs, goggles, pouches,
crowbars, corrugated fences, dry grass and scrap piles are generated by the builder.
The fixed portrait camera is closer (orthographic size 11.5) for readability.
Warm key light, cool fill and a local URP grading Volume define the palette.
Tire imprints use one 640-stamp mesh pool, fading over 14 seconds; restart clears
it. Four wheel particle emitters use soft dust textures, capped at 40 particles each.
Small arena dressing is combined by material. Shared materials enable instancing.
Controls and the physical WheelCollider setup are retained.
Backup before the art pass: Documentation/RoadGunner/Backups/Before-Scrap-Rush-2026-10-05.zip.

CONTROLS
W / A / D (and arrows): forward travel direction relative to the fixed camera.
S / Down arrow: reverse along the current chassis heading. S+A / S+D steer in reverse.
Mouse drag / touch in the lower-left region: floating analog joystick.
Joystick magnitude controls desired speed. Active joystick takes priority.
Space: service brake. R / RECOVER: upright reset at nearest clear recovery point.
Escape / II: pause. F3: telemetry (off by default). Restart resets the entire run.
The turret finds unobstructed targets within the visible 10 m ground circle and
fires automatically. There is no fire button. Cover blocks both vehicles' bullets.
Joystick direction always points the nose toward that direction on screen (360 degrees).
Pulling down turns the car toward the bottom of the screen and drives forward.
Only keyboard S / Down arrow selects reverse; a held joystick overrides these keys.
Changing between forward and reverse first brakes existing momentum.
Reverse speed is limited to 7 m/s (about 25 km/h); the speed display shows R.
Release input to coast. Use Recover if blocked or overturned (3-second cooldown).
Scrap is a session score; there is no progression, inventory, victory timer or save.

ENEMY VEHICLE — RUST VULTURE (2026-10-07)
Both RoadGunner_Prototype and Level01_Wasteland contain three pooled enemy
buggies, with editable spawn markers under SpawnDirector. They share the
existing 30-hostile population cap and are added alongside the raider groups.
Rust-colored armor, exposed roll cage and suspension, chunky tires, rear engine,
red bandanna driver and toothed ram match the Scrap Rush cartoon wasteland.
Each buggy drives using a Rigidbody and four WheelColliders. NavMesh paths guide
its steering; obstacle probes and a short reverse maneuver help avoid being stuck.
It approaches within 26 m, slows down before a firing position about 7.5 m
from the player and brakes to shoot. At less than 5.5 m it retreats. Navigation
selects clear firing positions around cover. Enemy vehicle collisions deal no
attack damage; the buggy has no ram-attack callback.
A rusty roof machine gun swivels toward the player and fires at 2 rounds/s,
dealing 5 damage per hit. Orange tracers and a muzzle flash identify enemy fire.
Both vehicle weapons use the same 10 m horizontal centre-to-centre radius from
Combat and population.asset. The player's pale ground circle follows the terrain
and uses that exact setting. A blocked sightline prevents firing through cover.
Spawn/resume warmup is 0.8 s; pause, defeat and destruction stop enemy fire.
Destruction triggers a bright cartoon blast, orange fire, dust, soot, sparks,
flying rusty debris, a ground pressure ring and a short original explosion sound.
Three reusable explosion slots expire after 2.4 s; restart clears them.
Destroyed armor is blackened until the next pooled spawn.
The automatic turret and player's ram can damage it. Destroying a buggy awards
one kill and three units of scrap; a smoking wreck returns to the pool after 2.5 s.
Respawn waits 16 s and requires an offscreen location at least 20 m from the player.
Settings/Enemy buggy.asset controls health, speed, aggro, firing distance,
retreat/braking, gun damage and delays.
Player attack radius is a single reusable transparent mesh with no collider;
its ground sampling excludes prop tops and its depth test respects obstacles.
Tools > Road Gunner > Enemies > Add Rust Vulture To Gameplay Scenes installs it
into the named scenes without duplicating existing buggy pools. Build Prototype
also includes it. Tools > Road Gunner > Validate > Run Enemy Buggy Checks runs
opt-in Play Mode diagnostics. See Documentation/RoadGunner/ENEMY_BUGGY.txt.

TUNING
Settings/Vehicle handling.asset:
- totalDriveTorque and torqueBySpeed: acceleration / top-end power;
- maxSpeed: 18 m/s forward limit; maxReverseSpeed: 7 m/s reverse limit;
- reverseTorqueMultiplier: reverse acceleration; rearTorqueShare: rear-biased AWD;
- steeringBySpeed, steeringRate: turning radius and steering response;
- serviceBrakeTorque, coastBrakeTorque, holdBrakeTorque: braking and coast;
- centerOfMass, spring, damper, suspensionTravel, antiRoll: suspension / stability;
- forwardFriction and sidewaysFriction: independent WheelCollider tire slip curves.
Settings/Combat and population.asset:
- range=10 m shared by player, buggy guns and player ground indicator;
- shotsPerSecond, bulletDamage, yawSpeed and aimTolerance for the player gun;
- enemyHealth, moveSpeed, aggroRadius, meleeReach and attackCooldown;
- activeCap=30, pickupCap=60, groupSize=4, replenishDelay=10.
Changing pool capacities requires rebuilding the prototype.
The camera's orthographicSize and FollowCamera damping are editable in the scene.

REBUILD
Tools > Road Gunner > Build Prototype regenerates only the named prototype scene,
its named generated materials/meshes/navigation data and prefabs, including the
Scrap Rush visual pass (Editor/ScrapRushArt.cs). Save other
modified scenes first. The builder refuses to discard an unrelated dirty scene.
It preserves tuning asset values. Edits to generated scene art are overwritten;
copy the scene or prefab before making a hand-authored variant.
Two consecutive rebuilds produce one camera, one EventSystem, one session and
identical object counts. The normal saved scene does not execute the builder.
All assets, sounds and low-poly meshes were generated locally; no paid assets,
external model generators or new packages are required.

TECHNICAL NOTES
One 1400 kg Rigidbody, four WheelColliders, wheel pose synchronization, bounded
anti-roll forces, rear-biased AWD. No transform-driven normal vehicle movement.
Fixed 0.02 s physics; interpolated Rigidbody; continuous dynamic collision.
100 x 100 m static terrain mesh, obstacle colliders and baked AI Navigation data.
30 raiders, 60 scrap objects, 12 tracers and 16 impacts are preallocated.
Excess scrap rewards merge into existing stacks. Raiders do not physically push
the car. A separate front trigger handles bounded ramming with per-target cooldown.
Hitscan uses actual muzzle direction and first hit; world geometry blocks shots.
Engine, gun, impact and pickup WAV files are original procedural sounds.
UI uses InputSystemUIInputModule, a 1080 x 1920 reference Canvas and SafeArea.
Mobile orientation is portrait; landscape autorotation is disabled.

LEVEL EDITOR (2026-10-09)
Tools > Road Gunner > Level Editor opens the in-editor level authoring window.
LEVEL: Create New Level / open a scene / Prepare Current Gameplay Scene / Save /
Save As / Place Player Spawn / Play Level.
ENEMIES and OBSTACLES: choose an existing prefab, press Place, click Scene View.
Escape cancels. Use normal Unity Move/Rotate/Duplicate/Delete and Undo.
Select an enemy and Create per-instance LEVEL OVERRIDE to edit native tuning;
DEFAULT VALUE preserves shared game settings. Optional existing encounter and
vehicle spawn markers are also available.
TERRAIN: Raise / Lower / Smooth / Flatten directly on the existing mesh ground,
with radius, strength, target world height and Undo. Save/Play bakes navigation.
VALIDATION checks references, bounds, navigation and collisions.
LevelEditor_Demo.unity is a saved working example. Full usage, architecture,
modified/new scripts, tests and limitations: Documentation/RoadGunner/LEVEL_EDITOR.txt.
