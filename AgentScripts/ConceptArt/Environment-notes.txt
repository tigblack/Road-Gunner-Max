Environment family
All 20 existing WastelandRestored prop kinds are rebuilt as actual3D meshes in their original prefab roots. The builder retains historical colliders, transforms, and gameplay components. The family includes folded/corrugated ivory/rust sheet metal, braced structures, empty fuel pump with nozzle/hose, tire stacks with raised tread, drums and pressed jerrycans, timber/steel supply crates, scavenged machinery, open-cabin pickup wreck, large bus wreck, utility poles, gates, wind pump, irregular sandstone, and restrained cactus/dry scrub.

Wreck quarter-panels have real scalloped tire openings; cabin glazing is framed/open. Rust and paint remnants are broad14-point eroded silhouettes. Roof corrosion follows folded sheet edges and includes a few larger blooms and joining seams. Shared ArtKit materials and batching/atlas are used; this agent does not change pipeline or lighting.

Reusable roadside dressing
ConceptDressing.Apply(level) creates8 compact shared-mesh prototype compositions and deterministically places up to100 instances along the5 existing route polylines. Reruns replace only the named Concept route-shoulder composition root. Everything added is noncolliding. Candidates near original obstacles, enemy anchors, intersections, workshop radius+4m, ground edges, or prior groups are rejected. Original routes, terrain mesh/elevations, dimensions, encounters, colliders, and NavMesh remain unchanged.

Final shoulder origins (per root review): main7.4m, western7.5m, eastern7.2m, bothspurs6.5m from route center. Prototype content extends chiefly outward from origin so small clusters become visible at the edges of the preserved18m gameplay-camera width while retaining a broad clear central corridor. Group spacing12.1–13.3m; actual count is returned by Apply, depending on exclusion tests.
