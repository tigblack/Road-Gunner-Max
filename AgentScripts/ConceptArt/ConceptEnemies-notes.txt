Concept enemies and loot visual authoring

Entry points in RoadGunner.ConceptArt.Editor.ConceptEnemies:
  ApplyRaider(Raider raider, int variant = 0)
  ApplyBuggy(EnemyBuggy buggy)
  ApplyPickup(GameObject pickup)
  RefreshAtlas()

ApplyRaider variants: 0 = narrow scavenger with crowbar; 1 = broader armored enforcer
with spiked club and helmet spike; 2 = heavy veteran with reinforced abdomen/back,
broad shoulder pads and salvage hammer. Choose variants from existing encounter
roles/tuning rather than changing stats. Root scale and CapsuleCollider stay intact.
Torso, visual, arms and legs are reconnected to the existing procedural animation.
Mask/helmet top is around 1.92 m; the small enforcer helmet spike reaches 2.01 m.
The ordinary humanoid remains within the original practical hitbox silhouette.

The builder writes reusable Raider_*_Model.prefab assets under ArtKit.Root/Prefabs.
All body/limb meshes use a shared color + metallic/smoothness atlas populated from
ArtKit.Mat. This produces exactly 5 MeshRenderers per raider. The rig meshes share
assets between pooled clones. No skinning, runtime mesh construction or additional
MonoBehaviour is introduced. RefreshAtlas() resamples palette materials after an
art-direction color/roughness adjustment; it does not rebuild geometry.

ApplyBuggy retains all WheelColliders, physics root, collider list, gun component,
yaw, pitch, muzzle and flash transforms. It removes the old visual parts around
those retained pivots and makes the new red-brown armored scout body and correctly
axis-aligned chunky tires. Wheel radius is taken from each existing WheelCollider.
The gun barrel terminates before the original unchanged muzzle position. New body
renderer becomes the existing armor flash target. Dust/smoke are left for global
effects styling. No AI, tuning, spawn markers or wheel setup is changed.

ApplyPickup preserves Metal gear and Scrap plates Transform identities and active
states. The cog has a real center hole, broad teeth, chamfered thickness and discrete
oxidized sectors. The legacy scrap reward view becomes a salvaged armor/bracket/shaft
cluster. Reward values, drop motion, radius and harpoon ownership are untouched.

Integration: Apply to editable prefab contents and to unpacked scene/pool objects.
Replacing old prefab-owned children directly on connected instances can be rejected
by Unity; unpacking first avoids that. Existing component references and colliders
must not be removed. This file was authored without editor/CLI mutation; root owns
application, Console checks, actual gameplay and visual screenshot iteration.
